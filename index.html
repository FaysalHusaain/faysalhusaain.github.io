My Toolbox

GoHighLevel

CRM, funnels & workflows
This is home base. I have been in it for years and there is not much I have not built with it. Funnels, pipelines, calendars, the whole automation layer, white label setups. When someone needs a system that actually runs their follow up, this is where I build it.

ClickFunnels

Funnels & migrations
I built a lot of funnels here before I moved most of my work into GoHighLevel, so I know it inside out. These days it is mostly migrations, moving people off ClickFunnels without losing their pages, their flows or their data.

Make

App to app automation
When a job needs two apps talking to each other in a way GoHighLevel cannot do on its own, this is what I reach for. Booking tools, sheets, payment platforms, whatever it is, I wire them together here.

Zapier

Quick integrations
The quick and reliable way to connect apps when a job does not need anything heavy. Good for the small automations that just need to work in the background and never be thought about again.

n8n

Custom automation
For the more custom automation work where I want full control and no per task limits. I reach for it when a build gets complex enough that the off the shelf tools start getting in the way.

ManyChat

Chat automation
For businesses that live in the DMs. I set up the chat flows that qualify people, answer the common questions, and hand the warm ones into the CRM without anyone sitting there replying all day.

Stripe

Payments & checkout
Payments and checkout, wired straight into the funnel. Subscriptions or one off, connected so the money side just works and updates the CRM on its own.

Webhooks & APIs

Custom connections
When something does not have a ready made connector, I build the connection myself. This is the glue that lets me join almost anything to almost anything when the standard tools run out.
